## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep sky blue (#00BFFF). The concept of education for all often incorporates the feeling of endless open skies of possibility. It provides a calming, trustworthy backdrop.
- Background color: Very light blue (#EBF4FA). The light tint of the analogous hue to the deep sky blue creates a background with high readability for accessibility purposes.
- Accent color: Electric blue (#7DF9FF). High contrast against background. For interactive elements to be vibrant.
- Font pairing: 'Playfair' (serif) for headings, for its balance of tradition and modernity in its high contrast look, with 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for body text.
- Use line-style icons to represent different sections and functionalities for a clean, modern look. Match the line weight and style across all icons.
- Employ a responsive grid system to ensure content is well-organized and adapts seamlessly to various screen sizes.
